## Figure 2.1 – Iterative 3-Step Risk Reduction Process (AS 4024.1301)

**Process applies separately to each hazard/condition of use.**
**At each iterative step: perform risk estimation, evaluation, and comparison.**

### Main Loop
1. Determine machine limits
2. Identify hazards
3. Estimate risk
4. Evaluate risk
   - Risk adequately reduced? → **YES** → END (check: are other hazards generated?)
   - Risk adequately reduced? → **NO** → continue below

### Decision Path
- Can the hazard be **removed**? → YES → back to top
- Can risk be reduced by **inherently safe design**? → YES → Step 1
- Can risk be reduced by **guards/protective devices**? → YES → Step 2
- Can the **limits be re-specified**? → NO → Step 3 / YES → back to top

### Step 1 – Inherently Safe Design Measures
- Intended risk reduction achieved? → YES → back to main loop
- NO → proceed to Step 2

### Step 2 – Safeguarding & Complementary Risk Controls
- Intended risk reduction achieved? → YES → back to main loop
- NO → proceed to Step 3

### Step 3 – Information for Use
- Intended risk reduction achieved? → YES → back to main loop
- NO → back to main loop (first answer based on initial risk assessment)
